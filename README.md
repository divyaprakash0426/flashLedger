## What is `flashLedger`?

`flashLedger` is an ultra-fast, sub-100ms bank feed categorizer and tax auditor. It maps raw financial statement feeds (e.g. `SQ *BLUE BOTTLE SOMA`, `AMZN Mktp US*284J1`, `GITHUB*SPONSOR 877-448`) to standard General Ledger (GL) Chart of Accounts (COA) with tax-deductibility flags, CapEx threshold enforcement, and audit risk scoring.
